How It Works

Three simple steps to sell your Louisiana home for cash — fast, easy, and stress-free.

1

Submit Your Property Details

Fill out our simple online form with your property address and basic information, or give us a call at (337) 270-0518. It takes less than 60 seconds.

You don't need to clean, repair, or even stage your home. We buy properties in any condition across all 64 Louisiana parishes — as-is.

What you'll need: Property address, your contact info, and a rough idea of the property condition. That's it!
2

Receive Your Fair Cash Offer

Within 24 hours of receiving your property details, one of our local Louisiana home buying specialists will contact you with a fair, no-obligation cash offer.

Our offer is based on comparable sales in your area, the current condition of your home, and local market data. We're completely transparent about how we arrive at our number — no surprises, no lowballing games.

No obligation: You're under zero pressure to accept. Take your time, ask questions, and decide what's best for you.
3

Close & Get Your Cash

Once you accept our offer, we schedule closing at a reputable local title company. You choose the closing date — we can close in as few as 7 days, or we can wait until a date that's convenient for you.

At closing, we handle all the paperwork. You walk away with cash in hand — via certified check or wire transfer. No waiting, no delays. We also pay ALL closing costs so there are zero deductions from your offer.

You keep 100% of the offer: No real estate commissions (saves you 5–6%), no closing costs (saves another 1–3%), no repair costs. What we offer is what you get.
