You Don't Have to Pay Off the Lien Before Selling

This is the part most homeowners don't realize: liens get paid off out of the sale proceeds at closing, not before. You don't need cash on hand to clear a contractor's lien, a judgment, or a tax lien before you can sell. The title company or closing attorney pays the lienholder directly from the proceeds, and you receive whatever equity is left over.

The challenge is that many traditional buyers and their lenders get nervous about properties with liens, which can slow down or kill a deal. We don't have that hesitation — we buy with liens in place all the time and factor the payoff directly into our offer and closing process.

Types of Liens We Help Homeowners Resolve

  • Mechanics/contractor liens — from unpaid repair or renovation work
  • Tax liens — unpaid property or income taxes attached to the home
  • Judgment liens — from a lawsuit or court ruling
  • HOA liens — unpaid dues or fines from a homeowners association
  • Second mortgages or HELOCs — additional debt secured against the property
  • Multiple stacked liens — more than one lien from different sources

Frequently Asked Questions — Selling a House With Liens in Louisiana

Do I need money upfront to pay off the lien? +
No. The lien is paid directly from the sale proceeds at closing — you don't need to come up with funds beforehand.
What if there's more than one lien on the property? +
We can still help. Multiple liens just get paid out in order of priority at closing. We'll work with the title company to sort out exactly how the proceeds get distributed.
What if the lien is worth more than my house? +
This is a more complex situation, but it's still worth a call. Depending on the lienholder, sometimes a negotiated payoff or short sale arrangement is possible. We'll talk through your specific numbers with you.
Can a contractor's lien really stop me from selling? +
It can complicate a traditional sale since most retail buyers' lenders won't move forward with an open lien. We don't have that restriction and can work through it directly with the title company.
How do I find out exactly what liens are on my property? +
A title search will show every recorded lien. As part of our process, we order a title search so both of us have full clarity before closing.
