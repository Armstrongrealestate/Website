Why Selling As-Is Makes Sense After Damage

After a major storm or fire, most homeowners face the same impossible situation: the insurance payout isn't enough to cover full repairs, contractors are booked out for months, and every day that passes the property deteriorates further — costing more in the long run.

Trying to repair a seriously damaged home before selling often means:

  • Spending $30,000–$100,000+ out of pocket or borrowing against assets you don't have
  • Waiting 6–18 months for contractors in a post-storm market where labor and materials are scarce
  • Dealing with permit issues, code compliance, and inspections that can derail the project
  • Still not recouping your full repair investment in the sale price

Selling as-is to a cash buyer skips all of that. You sell the home in its current condition, we take on the repair burden, and you walk away with cash to start rebuilding your life.

What About My Insurance Claim?

Insurance situations after storm or fire damage in Louisiana can be incredibly complicated — especially after major events like Hurricanes Laura, Delta, and Ida left tens of thousands of homeowners fighting denied or underpaid claims.

Here's how we handle different insurance scenarios:

  • Claim already settled — you received your payout and don't want to deal with repairs. We buy the home as-is and you keep the insurance money.
  • Claim still open — in many cases, you can assign your open insurance claim to us at closing, or we work around it. We'll discuss the specifics of your situation.
  • Claim denied — a denied claim doesn't prevent a sale. We buy based on the property value and condition regardless of the insurance outcome.
  • No insurance — we buy uninsured damaged properties regularly. The offer reflects the condition but we never walk away just because there's no insurance.

Frequently Asked Questions — Damaged Properties in Louisiana

Do you buy homes that are completely destroyed? +
Yes — even total losses have land value and sometimes salvageable structure. We evaluate every property individually. Even if the home is a complete teardown, we may still make an offer based on the lot value and location.
What if my home has mold from flood damage? +
Mold is one of the most common issues we see in Louisiana flood-damaged homes. It does not prevent us from buying. We take on the remediation cost ourselves — you don't need to do anything before closing.
Can I sell a fire-damaged home in Louisiana without rebuilding? +
Absolutely. You are not required to rebuild or repair a fire-damaged home before selling it. We buy fire-damaged properties as-is — partial burns, total losses, smoke damage only — in any condition.
What if I still have a mortgage on the damaged home? +
Your existing mortgage gets paid off at closing from the sale proceeds, just like any other sale. You receive whatever is left after the payoff. Having a mortgage never prevents a sale.
Do you buy in rural Louisiana parishes too? +
Yes — all 64 parishes. Storm and fire damage doesn't just happen in cities. We buy damaged properties in rural areas, small towns, and unincorporated communities throughout Louisiana.
