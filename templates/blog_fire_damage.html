What About My Insurance Claim?

Every situation is different, and we can often work around an open insurance claim — sometimes helping you assign the claim at closing, other times structuring the sale so you keep the claim proceeds. The right approach depends on your policy and where you are in the process. Call us and we'll walk through your specific situation. In most cases, an open claim does not prevent a sale.

Yes — we buy homes with everything from minor smoke damage to total structural loss. Even if the house needs to be torn down, the land and location still have value, and we make an offer based on that.

In most cases, yes. We work with open claims regularly and can discuss the best way to handle yours — whether that's assigning the claim or structuring the sale around it. Call us and we'll explain your options.
