Why Selling As-Is Often Makes More Financial Sense

Here's something most sellers don't realize: the discount you take selling as-is is often less than what you'd spend on repairs. Let's say your home is worth $150,000 fully repaired and it needs $50,000 in work. You might get $110,000 selling as-is — but after spending $50,000 on repairs plus 6% commission on $150,000 ($9,000), you'd net $91,000 the hard way versus $110,000 the easy way.

Every situation is different, but in many cases the math favors selling as-is — especially when you factor in the time, stress, and uncertainty of a repair-and-list strategy.
