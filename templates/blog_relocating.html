The Problem With Listing When You're Relocating

When you're relocating, listing your home traditionally creates a specific set of problems that don't exist for someone who's staying local:

  • You're managing the sale from a distance — coordinating showings, repairs, and negotiations while living somewhere else is exhausting
  • You may be paying two housing costs — your new place plus your Louisiana mortgage, taxes, insurance, and utilities
  • The home may sit vacant — vacant homes deteriorate faster, attract problems, and can affect your insurance coverage
  • Buyers sense urgency and negotiate harder — when agents tell buyers you've already moved, offers come in lower
  • Louisiana's market can be slow — depending on your area and price range, homes can sit 60–120 days
