Our Story

We're Louisiana Locals Who Care

Armstrong Buys Houses was founded with one simple mission: to give Louisiana homeowners a better, faster, fairer way to sell their homes. We saw too many people stuck — unable to sell because of repairs they couldn't afford, agents who didn't deliver, or timelines that didn't work for their lives.

So we built something different. A direct, honest, cash-buying service that puts the homeowner first — no games, no pressure, no hidden costs.

Since 2020, we've helped over 100+ Louisiana homeowners sell their homes quickly and walk away with cash in hand. We're proud to be a trusted part of the Louisiana community.
