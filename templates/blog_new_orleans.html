New Orleans Real Estate — What Sellers Need to Know

The New Orleans market is active but complex. Historic designation in neighborhoods like the French Quarter, Marigny, and Garden District can make renovations expensive and heavily regulated. Many older New Orleans homes have foundation issues related to the city's below-sea-level geography, outdated electrical systems like knob-and-tube wiring, and long histories of flood exposure.

These factors make it harder to sell to traditional buyers who need financing — lenders are strict about property condition and flood zone status. We buy with cash, so none of those issues prevent a sale.
