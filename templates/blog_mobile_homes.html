Why Most Buyers Won't Touch Mobile Homes — And Why We Do

The challenge with mobile homes is financing. Most traditional buyers need a mortgage, and conventional mortgage lenders won't finance mobile homes — especially older ones. FHA and VA loans have strict requirements about age, foundation type, and condition. This leaves mobile home sellers with a very small pool of buyers.

We buy with cash, so there's no lender involved. That's what makes it possible for us to purchase mobile homes that retail buyers simply can't finance. For 2010 and newer homes with vinyl siding, the value is there and the transaction is straightforward.

Mobile Homes on Owned Land vs. Rented Lots

Whether your mobile home sits on land you own or on a rented lot in a park makes a difference in how the transaction works — but either way we can often help.

Mobile home on owned land: This is the simplest situation. The home and land are typically sold together, title transfers cleanly, and the transaction works very similarly to a traditional home sale.

Mobile home on a rented lot: This is more common in Louisiana mobile home parks. The home is personal property (titled like a vehicle) rather than real estate. We can still purchase the home — the title transfers separately and the lot lease is either terminated or transferred to us. We coordinate with the park management to handle this smoothly.

Frequently Asked Questions — Selling Mobile Homes in Louisiana

What if my mobile home is a 2009 or older? +
Unfortunately we can only purchase mobile homes that are 2010 model year or newer with vinyl siding. Older homes have more limited resale options which affects our ability to buy. Call us anyway and we'll let you know honestly if there's anything we can do.
Does the mobile home have to be on a permanent foundation? +
Not necessarily. We buy mobile homes on owned land and on rented lots in parks. Foundation type is a factor in our valuation but not a hard requirement.
What if I still owe money on my mobile home? +
Just like a regular home sale, your existing loan gets paid off from the sale proceeds at closing. You receive whatever remains after the payoff.
How is a mobile home title transferred in Louisiana? +
Mobile homes not permanently affixed to owned land are titled as personal property through the Louisiana Office of Motor Vehicles — similar to a vehicle title. We handle all the paperwork for you.
Can you close fast on a mobile home sale? +
Yes — mobile home transactions can often close faster than traditional real estate because there's less title work involved. In many cases we can close within 7–14 days.
